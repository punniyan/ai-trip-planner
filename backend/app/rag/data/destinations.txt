DUBAI

Dubai is a popular travel destination in the United Arab Emirates.

Top attractions in Dubai include Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall, Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Marina, Dubai Frame, Museum of the Future, Jumeirah Mosque, and the Dubai Fountain.

Burj Khalifa is the world's tallest building and is located in Downtown Dubai.

Dubai Mall is one of the world's largest shopping malls and is located near Burj Khalifa.

Palm Jumeirah is a man-made island known for luxury hotels, restaurants, beaches, and resorts.

Dubai Marina is known for waterfront restaurants, hotels, shopping, and evening activities.

The Museum of the Future is an architectural landmark in Dubai.

Dubai has a hot desert climate. The period from November to March is generally more comfortable for outdoor sightseeing.

Popular activities in Dubai include desert safari, dune bashing, sightseeing, shopping, visiting beaches, cruises, and exploring cultural attractions.

Dubai uses the United Arab Emirates dirham (AED).

SINGAPORE

Singapore is a modern city-state in Southeast Asia.

Popular attractions include Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, Sentosa Island, Merlion Park, Singapore Zoo, Universal Studios Singapore, and Chinatown.

Singapore is known for its clean urban environment, public transportation, shopping, food, and cultural attractions.

BANGKOK

Bangkok is the capital city of Thailand.

Popular attractions include the Grand Palace, Wat Arun, Wat Pho, Chatuchak Market, and Chinatown.

Bangkok is known for Thai food, temples, markets, shopping, nightlife, and cultural experiences.

PARIS

Paris is the capital of France.

Popular attractions include the Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, Arc de Triomphe, Notre-Dame area, Sacré-Cœur, and Champs-Élysées.

Paris is known for museums, architecture, food, fashion, history, and culture.
